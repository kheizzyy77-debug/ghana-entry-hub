The Legal Due Diligence Matrix

Before any building project or land is added to our list, it must clear our strict three-step verification process.

01. Lands Commission Validation

We check property deeds directly at the Lands Commission to confirm land boundaries and ensure there are no ownership arguments or hidden legal disputes.

02. Local Permits Audit

We verify that all layouts have official building approvals, environment certifications, and fully match local town planning rules.

03. Developer Reliability Check

We thoroughly check construction companies to confirm their past build timelines and delivery records, completely removing your investment risks.
